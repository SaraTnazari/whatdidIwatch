Ever spent 20 minutes trying to remember a movie? You know the plot. You remember that one scene. You can picture the actor's face. But the title? Gone. You try Google. Nothing. You try IMDb with five different keywords. Still nothing.

What Did I Watch? is an AI-powered movie identifier that solves this. Describe what you remember in your own words and our AI figures out exactly what you're thinking of. No keywords needed. No browsing lists. Describe it like you would to a friend and get your answer in seconds.

How it works:

Open the app, type or speak your description, and tap Find It. Say something like "animated movie where a rat becomes a chef in Paris" or "show where a chemistry teacher starts cooking meth" or even "a cartoon I watched as a kid where kids battle with spinning tops." Our AI understands context, plot descriptions, character traits, and the way people naturally remember things. You get up to five ranked matches, each with the movie poster, title, release year, ratings, a plot summary, and an explanation of why the AI thinks it matches.

Every result includes direct links to where you can watch it right now on JustWatch, Apple TV, Amazon Prime Video, YouTube, and more. No more searching across ten streaming apps.
