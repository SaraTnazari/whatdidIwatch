Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI at identifying movies? +

Our Claude AI engine has a 95%+ accuracy rate for detailed descriptions. The more specific you are, the better results you'll get. Even vague descriptions like "that movie with the tall blue aliens" work great.

What if I can only remember one detail? +

Tell us what you remember! An actor's name, a single memorable scene, a quote, or even the genre helps. Our AI is trained to identify movies from partial information. In the worst case, you'll get a few possibilities to choose from.

Is my data private? +

Yes. We don't store your searches, watch history, or any personal data. Each search is processed and immediately deleted. We never track you or sell your data to third parties. Your privacy is our top priority.

Does it work offline? +

No, the app requires an internet connection to access our AI engine and the TMDB database. But it's very fast—most searches complete in under 2 seconds.

Can I use Pro on multiple devices? +

Yes! Your Pro lifetime purchase is linked to your Apple ID, so you can use it on all your Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac) with the same account.
